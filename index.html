I am an MS by Research student in Computer Science and Engineering at CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad, advised by Prof. C. V. Jawahar. Alongside my MS, I lead VISIA at INAI, IIIT Hyderabad, taking adverse-weather restoration from research to edge deployment.

Previously, I was a Research Intern at SAC, ISRO, where I built an ML surrogate for radiative transfer models of greenhouse gases that runs about 750× faster, and at DIAT, DRDO, where I worked on deep learning–based leak detection. I received my B.Tech in Information Technology from Dharmsinh Desai University.

My work has been published at ICVGIP, OTCON, and ERCICAM and in Springer CCIS, and received a Best Paper Award at ASCIS 2023. I also have a published Indian patent application for a real-time text-to-Braille converter.

Research

My research explores how adverse visual conditions such as fog, rain, and low illumination affect visual representations and downstream perception, and how image and video restoration can improve their reliability. I also work on physically grounded degradation modelling, domain adaptation, and efficient inference.

Areas of Interest
  • Image and Video Restoration
  • Degradation Modelling
  • Domain Adaptation
  • Knowledge Distillation
  • Efficient Inference
  • Visual SLAM

Some papers are highlighted.

ICVGIP 2025

Enhancing Driving Visibility via Semantic-Guided Knowledge Distillation Framework for Adverse Weather Removal

H. S. Mukkamala, S. Gangisetty, Ananya Vivek Kulkarni, V. G. Yalla, C. V. Jawahar

We propose a semantic-guided knowledge distillation framework that improves visibility restoration under adverse weather while enabling efficient real-time deployment on edge devices.

paper code

OTCON 2025

Improving Vehicle Visibility in Fog Environment

Ananya Vivek Kulkarni, Zankhana Barad, Harshadkumar B. Prajapati

This work combines image enhancement techniques (DCP, CLAHE) with deep learning-based detection and tracking to improve vehicle visibility and robustness in foggy driving scenarios.

paper

ERCICAM 2025

Gender Agreement in Indo-Aryan Languages Using Rule-Based Parsing and Finite Automata

Ananya Vivek Kulkarni, Srushti Pednekar, Deepak Vegda

We present a rule-based syntactic parsing framework using finite automata to model gender agreement in Indo-Aryan languages, combining linguistic theory with computational efficiency.

paper

Springer CCIS 2024 Best Paper Award

Raspberry Pi-Driven Affordable Image-to-Braille Converter for Visually Impaired Users

Ananya Vivek Kulkarni, Maitri Shah, Nivedita Thakur, Srushti Pednekar, Viral H. Shah

We propose a low-cost, real-time image-to-Braille conversion system using OCR and Raspberry Pi, designed to improve accessibility to educational content for visually impaired users.
